# DEEPWAKE feature audit

`GOAL.md` is the product specification. This document lists every feature it asks for and every validation area of its §20, with the files that build it and the checks that verify it. Nothing in `GOAL.md` is `Missing` or `Broken`.

How to read the table:

- **Spec source** is a section of `GOAL.md`.
- A name in double quotes is a test name. `node --test --test-reporter=spec tests/<file>` prints it. `npm test` runs all of them.
- `check:browser` with a letter is one check of `npm run check:browser`, named as in `tools/browser-check.mjs`.
- `tools/preview.mjs` gives an image. An image is not an assertion.
- **Status** is `Working` when a named check asserts the behavior. It is `Partial` when the feature is built and part of it has no named check.

## Verification results

- `npm test`: 308 tests, 308 pass, 0 fail. Run for this document.
- `npm run check:browser`: 13 of 13 checks passed in its last recorded run, in 134.56 s. It was not re-run for this document.
- The numbers of the browser check and the way each was taken are in `README.md`, "Measurements".

## Deliberate deviations

Every place where the game differs from a number or a rule of `GOAL.md`.

1. **Route length.** §10 asks for "approximately 180–240 units of route length".
   - Measured: the course spans x 0 to 238 and y −2 to 84 (`LEVEL.bounds`). The spawn is at (4.5, 6) and the lift console at (229.5, 66): 225 units east and 60 up. The scripted main route (`MAIN` in `tests/route-steps.js`) moves ILO 467.33 units in 16479 steps: 327.67 horizontal (277.84 east, 49.84 west) and 215.29 vertical (146.64 up, 68.64 down).
   - How: a one-off Node command for this document played `MAIN` with `Driver` and summed the distance ILO moved per step. No test or tool computes a route length.
   - Reason: the route climbs through five wall-jump shafts and walks back west twice, so the walked path is about twice the width of the course.
   - Decision: kept. The width of the course, 238 units, lies inside the range of §10. The walked path does not.
2. **Duration of a first playthrough.** §1 asks for "approximately 8–12 minutes".
   - Measured: the scripted clean run ends at clock 119.82 s. The run with all three archives ends at 137.26 s (browser check J). No person has played the game for a measurement.
   - Reason: the script knows the course and waits only where a mechanism makes it wait.
   - Decision: not shown. No evidence meets or misses the target.
3. **Name of the pulse ability.** §8 calls it "Echo pulse".
   - Measured: the control lists and the label of the energy meter say `Echo Pulse`. The touch button and the HUD chip say `Pulse` (`src/ui/ui.js`).
   - Reason: not recorded. The four action buttons carry one-word names: `Jump`, `Dash`, `Pulse`, `Use`.
   - Decision: kept. With touch controls the pause menu lists `Pulse` as the key and `Echo Pulse` as the action.
4. **`label` on sockets and switches.**
   - Measured: `src/level/build.js` gives every socket and switch a `label`: `Circuit`, `Lift`, `Relay`, `Bridge`, `Latch`. The player never sees it.
   - Reason: the comment of the file says the label names the object for the tests. No rule of `GOAL.md` concerns it.
   - Decision: kept. It is listed here because a reader of the level data may take it for a player-facing name.
5. **Reach of `E`.** §11 says "within approximately 1.3 units".
   - Measured: 0.8 units from ILO's body on every side (`PLAYER.interactPad` in `src/config.js`, `inRange` in `src/sim/objects.js`). For a cell beside ILO that is 1.675 between the two centers.
   - Reason: not recorded.
   - Decision: kept.
6. **No `Q — Echo Pulse` prompt.** §17 lists it as an example of a contextual prompt.
   - Measured: the prompts are `Pick Up Cell`, `Place Cell`, `Insert Cell`, `Remove Cell`, `Activate Switch`, `Salvage Cache`, `Read Archive` and `Activate Lift` (`src/sim/objects.js`). None asks for a pulse.
   - Reason: not recorded.
   - Decision: kept. §17 says "such as", so the prompt is an example, not a rule.
7. **The passage revealed by a pulse lies on the main route.** §10 lists "a pulse-revealed side passage" for the Crystal Reservoir, and its route rules say that a mandatory path cannot depend on a secret.
   - Measured: the hidden seam `resPassage` (x 97, y 13 to 16 in `LEVEL.seams`) is on the main route. Step `M15` of `tests/route-steps.js` pulses and dashes east through it, and step `M16` follows in the switch shaft. A one-off command for this document broke the seam with a dash from x 96 and no pulse before it (events `dash`, `seamBreak`).
   - Reason: not recorded.
   - Decision: kept. The path depends on no secret. The seam is drawn as the rock around it with a faint amber tint (`src/render/terrain.js`), a dash breaks it with or without a pulse, and the pulse only highlights it for 5 s.
8. **Beam burst of the Sentinel.** §12 says "approximately 0.9-second".
   - Measured: 0.8 s (`SENTINEL.firing` in `src/sim/enemies.js`).
   - Reason: not recorded.
   - Decision: kept, inside the word "approximately".

## Not verified

- **Audio heard by a person.** Every audio check records calls on a fake AudioContext. The browser check says nothing about sound.
- **Touch on a physical device.** Check H sends emulated touch events to headless Chromium on an emulated phone.
- **Frame time on a real graphics card.** Check M ran on software WebGL (SwiftShader) only, and it has no pass condition.
- **Duration of a first playthrough.** No person has played the game for a measurement.
- **Bypassing every enemy.** The scripted run takes no hit. No check shows that a player can pass every machine.
- **The look of the game.** Colors, textures, models, lights and parallax are in images only: 29 PNGs in `dev/BrowserCheck/` and `tools/preview.mjs`. That includes the model of ILO and its poses.
- **Layout of the results card and of the archive fragment in a narrow viewport.** Check F covers the title, play and pause views.
- **Deep water of the course.** No check drops ILO into the two water rects. The recovery of a fall is checked on a fixture pit.
- **`cache2`.** No check collects it.
- **Softlocks.** No check searches for a state the player cannot leave.
- **Single values without a check of their own**: `DT`, the fall speed limit, the expansion time of the pulse, the clamp on the damage shake, the pixel ratio cap, the texture size, the reach of `E`, the body size of ILO.
